Paste the comments under a post, or give a Reddit link. Each comment is classified by a model trained on Vietnamese student forum posts, and the post's topic and sentiment are read from all its comments together.

The models were trained on NEU-ESC (Vietnamese student forum posts) or UIT-VSFC (course feedback sentences). Comments on other subjects or in other languages fall outside what they learned, so treat the answers as a rough guide. The post-level answer is the average of the comments' probabilities; "agreement" is the share of comments whose own top label matches it.
